Proving a document is real — in seconds.

AI document-authenticity and fraud detection for government schemes. Mathematics decides what mathematics can decide — and the system says plainly what it could not verify.

The problem

Aadhaar proves who you are.
It does not prove what your paper says.

Most welfare fraud in India is not a stolen identity. It is a real, correctly-authenticated person submitting a document that says something untrue about them.

Income certificate

says ₹96,500 — the bank statement says otherwise

Caste / domicile certificate

carries a seal the issuing officer never applied

Bank guarantee

backs a tender, and the bank has never heard of it

Checked how? One clerk, hundreds of applications, no forensic tooling, no time. A competent forgery passes. An honest applicant with an odd-looking document gets rejected. Both failures are invisible.

The solution

Upload a document. Get a defensible verdict.

  • Verdict — Authentic / Suspicious / Likely fake, with a 0–100 risk score
  • Red flags — each quoting the exact evidence on the document
  • Cross-field logic — does the arithmetic and the story hold up?
  • Identifier validation — Aadhaar, PAN, GSTIN, IFSC, IBAN, MRZ checksums
  • 12-module forensic dossier — printable, auditable
  • What it could NOT verify — stated explicitly, never hidden

How it works

Deterministic facts first. AI reasoning second.

The order is the product. Anything a computer can prove, a computer proves — and the AI is not permitted to argue with it.

The differentiator

We made the AI argue with itself

A single AI pass either cries fraud at every compression artifact, or waves everything through.

Prosecution

Builds the strongest honest case that it is forged — citing only observable evidence.

Defence

Gives the innocent explanation — scanner artifacts, recompression, a legitimate re-save — and exposes overreach.

Judge

Weighs both against binding code-computed facts. Records what was decisive — and what it dismissed.

That dismissal list is the false-positive guard — and the officer sees it. A failed checksum cannot be argued away; an OFAC list that failed to download cannot be spun into "clean".

The ask

One scheme. One department. Real documents.

A pilot gives us the two things we cannot generate ourselves: a labelled dataset to calibrate confidence against, and the sponsorship needed for DigiLocker issuer verification.

What we measure
  • Fraudulent documents caught that manual review missed
  • False positives — honest applicants wrongly flagged
  • Officer time per application
  • Turnaround, submission to decision
Where it sits
  • Counter-level triage — clerk uploads, gets a reason
  • Audit trail — every verdict stores its evidence
  • Officer decides. Pramaan recommends; it never auto-rejects
